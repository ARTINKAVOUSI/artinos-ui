# ARTINOS CONTINUOUS INSTRUMENT · SLIDER + RANGE

Slider

One compact precision surface. The capsule is the slider, the seam is the handle, the readout is the precision scrub zone. Every control below is live.

01 MATERIAL

A body press places the value and keeps dragging. A press within 13 px of the seam grabs it without a jump. The seam sits 4 px inside the active insert; text switches ink where the insert covers it.

02 PRECISION

Drag from the readout. Vertical distance from it selects the sensitivity; extra digits appear while scrubbing and collapse on release. The HUD exists only for this gesture.

03 PAIRED

Label outside the capsule. A layout variation only: material, interaction and precision are unchanged.

04 STEPPED

steps quantizes the value, not only the drawing. Cells for small counts; hairline ticks up to 16; nothing beyond.

05 DETENTS + MAPPING

Semantic points with a 6 px magnet; the seam lights on capture. Ctrl widens the magnet to 22 px, Ctrl + arrow jumps between detents. Log and dB mappings change geometry only; the value stays exact.

06 GRADIENT

The track carries the parameter's own scale. While dragging, a swatch reads out the sampled colour below the seam. Label and value ink follow the luminance under them.

07 RULER

The scale moves beneath a fixed index. Drag right and the scale follows the hand. Precision from the readout zooms the engraving: ticks spread and finer subdivisions fade in. Marked positions hold with a light magnet.

08 VISUALIZED

Data on the surface only when it describes the parameter: the response the value samples, the signal the playhead scrubs. The drawing is the interaction surface.

09 ENABLE / OFF

Double-click switches the parameter off; the stored value is kept and shown. Double-click again to restore it. An off slider still takes focus but ignores drag, wheel and keys.

Family

SAME WELL · SAME INSERT · SAME LED SEAM · SAME MATERIAL PROP
11 SWITCH

The slider insert as a two-position shutter. It travels to the active half and its LED seam lights; off, the same insert drains to grey. Click, Space or Enter.

12 TOGGLE GROUP

Independent flags sharing one well. Each chip is a small insert with its own LED seam.

13 SEGMENTED

One of n. The insert travels between positions and the text it arrives under switches ink. Arrow keys move it.

14 COLOR

Label in the well, the colour itself as the insert. The hex ink follows the colour's luminance. Click the swatch for the system picker.

15 LIST

Closed, the list is a full-width insert with a chevron. Open, it extends downward into a soft-light glass panel. Arrow keys, Enter and Escape work throughout.

16 PORTABLE

One file, ArtinosSlider.jsx. No ARTINOS runtime required: it reads role tokens when present and falls back to literals. No permanent animation loop; ticks and detents are a handful of nodes.
